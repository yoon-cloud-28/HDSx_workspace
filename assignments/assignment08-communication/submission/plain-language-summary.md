This analysis is for a public-health analyst preparing a short briefing on descriptive BMI patterns in the classroom NHANES equity dataset. The analysis shows that those with higher income have lower BMI compared to other income groups across NHANES cycles. Due to the limitations of the dataset, the result does not allow for general public representation and causal inference. Future analysis should use the actual NHANES dataset so that the survey weights can be used for analysis allowing the result to capture the a nationally representative estimates.
